Garlic Butter Pasta

This garlic butter pasta is a quick, comforting dish made with simple ingredients and big flavor. Perfect for busy weeknights or a cozy dinner.

Ingredients

  • Pasta (spaghetti or fettuccine)
  • Butter
  • Fresh garlic cloves
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and black pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until al dente.
  2. Melt butter in a pan and sauté minced garlic until fragrant.
  3. Drain the pasta and add it to the garlic butter.
  4. Toss well, season with salt and pepper, and top with parmesan.
